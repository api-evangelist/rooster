We’re thrilled to announce the next fellows for our Writer’s Residency in Downtown Las Vegas. We welcome our fellows Eric Tanyavutti, Liz Iversen, Jared Jackson, Mimi Lok, Isobel Harbison, and Krista Knight. Fellows will spend a month in the vibrant heart of downtown Las Vegas, engaging with and becoming a part of the city’s thriving arts scene.
