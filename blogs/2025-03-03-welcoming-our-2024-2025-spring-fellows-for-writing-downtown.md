We’re thrilled to announce the next fellows for our Writer’s Residency in Downtown Las Vegas. We welcome our fellows Emily Wilson, Naomi Krupitsky, John Sears, and guests Gladys Lu and Marie Nguyen. Fellows will spend a month in the vibrant heart of downtown Las Vegas, engaging with and becoming a part of the city’s thriving arts scene.
