Written by Morgan Canaan, Writing Atlas Fellow In early February, Plympton Story Atlas Fellows met with Helen Thomaides, Assistant Editor at W.W. Norton and former Plympton Fellow, to learn how aspiring editors and writers might breach the publishing industry’s gate-kept exterior. She detailed her career path and average day at work before graciously answering questions from Fellows.
